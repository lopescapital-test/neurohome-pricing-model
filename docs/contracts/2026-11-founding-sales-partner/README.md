# Founding Sales Partner agreement, November 2026

A draft 1099 sales agreement for NeuroSage Home's first independent sales partner, plus the internal memo that explains the pay design. Both are drafts pending attorney review. The partner's name, state and our signing entity are still placeholders.

> These files are public, like the rest of this repo. The memo is written for us, not for the partner: it shows our unit costs, our negotiating positions and the legal risks we see.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Founding_Sales_Partner_Agreement_DRAFT_23_11_2026.docx` | The agreement. Pay follows the plan agreed on November 20: 10% of commissionable revenue, with monthly volume accelerators (12% above $40k, 14% above $80k, 16% above $120k, marginal), an 18-month rate lock, and a 1.5% override on recruits' sales while both stay active. It also has client-quality gates, pro rata clawbacks, a 10% reserve, and Schedule E with 14 worked examples. |
| `Founding_Sales_Partner_Comp_Proposal_23_11_2026.docx` | The internal memo: why the accelerators and override are set where they are, what they cost us, the risks (worker classification, health care referral laws, lead source) and the decisions still open. |

Figures in both use the November 2026 prices in `model.js`, a 3% card fee and $950 of labs in the Clinical and Guided programs.
