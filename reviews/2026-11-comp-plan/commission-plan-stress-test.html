Proposed changes

Rates stay where the deck sets them. These change what the rate is paid on and add limits, so the plan holds on every product, not only the core programs.

Pay therapy plans on price minus pass-through costs

Labs, physician time, supplements included in a program and supplement credits are passed through at or near cost. Take them out before applying 15% / 6%.

Fixes the Pre-Intensive and the Dr. Jared bundle; trims about $140 of commission on labs from each Clinical sale.

Cap total pay on a sale at 25% of its gross profit

Commission plus bonuses on one sale never exceeds a quarter of what the sale earns before overhead. A safety net for products added later.

None of the nine examples hits the cap after rule 1; without rule 1, the Pre-Intensive and Dr. Jared bundle would.

No bundle bonus on any product that already includes equipment

The deck excludes Clinical, Guided and Wellness. Extend that to every product with a kit item inside it.

Removes the $250 / $125 bonus from the Pre-Intensive, which includes a green panel.

Give every product a rate, and make "none" the default

List the Dr. Jared bundle, Eva, care-team and physician time, Hub renewals and v1 plans by name.

Stops each one being argued as a 15% first therapy plan.

Pay supplements on gross profit, like equipment, and nothing below cost

20% / 10% of gross profit. A product sold at a loss pays no commission until it is repriced.

Same payout on normal supplements; AdrenaCalm pays $0 until its cost or price is fixed.
